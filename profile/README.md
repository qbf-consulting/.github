# QBF Consulting LLP

**Digital public infrastructure, trust, governance, and open knowledge.**

QBF Consulting LLP works at the intersection of digital systems, institutional governance, and public-interest technology. We help organisations translate complex policy and trust requirements into implementable architectures, specifications, governance models, and operational systems.

Our work focuses on digital trust infrastructure, digital identity and credentials, governance and assurance, agentic systems, interoperability, and digital public infrastructure. We are particularly interested in the point where governance stops being documentation and becomes an executable property of a system.

## Open knowledge

Open knowledge is a core part of how we work. Where appropriate, we develop reusable specifications, reference architectures, implementation patterns, research, tooling, and other public artifacts that allow ideas to be examined, tested, improved, and adopted beyond a single engagement.

We believe durable digital infrastructure benefits from transparent reasoning, inspectable governance, interoperable standards, and evidence that can be independently evaluated.

## What we work on

- Digital trust and trust infrastructure
- Digital identity, verifiable credentials, and decentralised identifiers
- Digital public infrastructure and public-interest technology
- Governance, risk, compliance, assurance, and policy-to-code
- Trust registries, directories, and discovery infrastructure
- Agentic AI governance, delegation, authority, and redress
- Interoperability architectures, specifications, and implementation guidance
- Open specifications, reference implementations, research, and knowledge infrastructure

## Our approach

We work across policy, governance, architecture, and implementation rather than treating them as separate layers. The objective is to make institutional intent survive contact with software: requirements should be testable, authority should be explicit, decisions should be auditable, and governance should have an operational enforcement surface.

This GitHub organisation hosts QBF Consulting's public technical work, open knowledge artifacts, specifications, reference implementations, experiments, and supporting documentation.

## Provenance and stewardship

QBF Consulting maintains projects that may originate in, compose with, or remain connected to independently developed technical work, standards activity, research, and open-source portfolios.

Repository ownership identifies **current institutional stewardship**. It does not imply exclusive authorship of underlying ideas, nor does it transfer authority from related specifications, projects, standards bodies, or independently governed repositories.

Where provenance or architectural relationships matter, individual repositories document those relationships explicitly. Normative authority, release authority, governance, and maintenance responsibility remain with the repository or institution that owns the relevant scope.

QBF projects may therefore participate in broader technical stacks and interoperability arrangements without those relationships implying common ownership or governance.
